## Made for this test slice
Map and trench network, every tree model (dead / snapped / burnt / living pines, stumps, logs, bushes, far-tree cards),
trench furniture (sandbags, plank / wattle / corrugated revetments, posts, duckboards, ladders, pickets, barbed wire,
concertina, crates, buckets, jerrycans, bridges), the three dugouts and their furniture, the power pylon and its cables,
the wood / burlap / metal / cloth / map textures, particle textures, sky clouds, every sound (synthesised), shaders and code.
